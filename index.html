Services

Design, checking and site work for concrete and steel buildings. Most of it happens online. Inspections need a site visit.

Service What you receive Where

Residential structural design

Villas, houses and apartment buildings in reinforced concrete, from foundations to roof.

 Structural model, calculation report, and drawings of foundations, columns, slabs and beams with reinforcement details. Online

Assessment and retrofitting

Cracks, corrosion, war or earthquake damage, or adding floors to an existing building.

 Site inspection, a condition report with photos, and a strengthening design using concrete jackets, steel or FRP. Site visit in Lebanon

Steel structures

Warehouses, canopies, mezzanines and solar panel mounting structures.

 Member and connection design, base plates and anchor bolts, and general arrangement drawings. Online

Design checking

An independent review of another engineer's design, for owners, contractors and developers.

 A review report listing each issue found, with the code reference and the fix we recommend. Online

Online consultation

Talk through a crack, a renovation, removing a wall, or a contractor's proposal.

 A 45-minute video call on Google Meet or WhatsApp, with a written summary afterwards. Online

Support for engineering offices

Analysis models, design calculations and drafting for offices in Lebanon and abroad.

 Model files, calculation sheets and drawings in your office's format, checked before delivery. Online

About AWDE Engineering

AWDE Engineering is a structural engineering practice led by Eng. Mohammad Awde. We work with architects, contractors and building owners to design new structures and assess existing ones.

We work online, so you get clear answers quickly wherever your project is. We keep structures simple and economical to build, without cutting anything that keeps them safe.

Every project gets

  • A structure checked for gravity, wind and earthquake loads
  • Calculations a reviewer can follow line by line
  • Drawings your contractor can build from
  • Direct contact with the engineer who designed it
